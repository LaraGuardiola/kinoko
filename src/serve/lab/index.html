Kinoko Lab

This page shows a kinoko agent at work. It needs the hub's permission, like any device.

On the computer running kinoko serve, run:

kinoko serve lab

and open the link it prints, or scan the QR code under the title that fits this device: AT HOME (this network) or ANYWHERE (Tailscale). Each link works once, for ten minutes.
